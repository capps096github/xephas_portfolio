- `/code/calc` and `/code` pricing, rate card v6: the estimate is now a range (low / likely / high) instead of one figure. The likely value is UGX 1,100,000 x (lines / 100,000)^0.744, plus a credit for history depth and review record of up to 35% in total: commits up to 18%, pull requests up to 15%, branches up to 4%. Each credit grows with the log of the count and is full at 1,000 / 100 / 10. The range is x0.79 to x1.26. The 50,000 and 100,000 rate jumps are gone, and price per line now falls as repos grow. The shape was fitted to the owner's 13 accepted outcomes and checked by leave-one-out (median / mean error: old card's shape 24.8% / 33.5%, lines only 17.1% / 19.8%, chosen 16.8% / 21.1%). By owner decision, the 100,000-line anchor is the level-neutral UGX 1,100,000, which matches rate card v5 on average over 30,000 to 1,000,000 lines (-0.9%). Also by owner decision, the commit and PR weights were raised from a draft 6% / 5% after comparing six weight sets. The data does not support them: ridge fitting sets commits to 0% and PRs to about 1%, and the largest outcome leads on every input at once. Their measured cost is +1.3 points mean leave-one-out error against lines only. Inputs are clamped (1,000 to 10,000,000 lines, 1 or more branches), and the 30,000-line eligibility is unchanged. What visitors see, likely value with no history: 30,000 lines UGX 300,000 -> 449,000; 100,000 1,200,000 -> 1,100,000; 250,000 2,700,000 -> 2,175,000; 500,000 3,950,000 -> 3,643,000; 1,000,000 6,450,000 -> 6,101,000. Repos with history get up to 35% more. So v6 is level-neutral against v5 only at zero history credit. A typical seller (median credit 12.2% across the 13 calibration repos) lands about 11% above v5 on average over 30,000 to 1,000,000 lines. Full history is up to about +100% over v5 at 30,000 lines and +24% at 100,000, and the largest absolute gap is about UGX +2.36M near 2.2M lines (checked with a scratch Node script; no behaviour change, stated in the `code_rate.ts` header too). `/code` now says "Estimates start from about UGX 449,000" (the likely value; the low end is 355,000 and the final price is negotiable), and its calculator card describes the range and the credit. The internal `?advanced` view shows a per-credit breakdown and the range, and the copied quote includes the history. The quality bonus is unchanged.
